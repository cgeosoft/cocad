Analytics

The site loads PostHog only after you accept. If you decline, the page makes no request to PostHog.

After you accept, PostHog records these events: page view, download click (system and file type) and demo prompt. It does not record your clicks in general, your keystrokes or your session. No event holds your name or your email address.

PostHog keeps the events for the retention period of the project. To ask for their removal, write to the contact address.

Change your choice

You can change your choice at any time. When you decline, the site stops the analytics and removes the PostHog ID from your browser.

The desktop app

SCAD Viewer makes no connection to a server of ours. It has no telemetry, no update check and no account. Your models stay on your computer.

The MCP server listens on 127.0.0.1 only. If you connect an AI client, that client sends what it reads to its own model provider. The policy of that provider applies.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, and for its correction or deletion. You can object to its use, and you can withdraw consent. To do this, write to the contact address. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
